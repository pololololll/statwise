01

What is Linear Regression?

Simple linear regression models the linear relationship between one independent variable (X) and one dependent variable (Y).

02

Understanding the Slope

The slope tells you how much the predicted Y changes when X increases by one unit. A positive slope is an increasing trend; a negative slope is a decreasing trend.

03

Understanding Pearson's r

Pearson's r describes the strength and direction of a linear relationship. The sign gives direction; the magnitude is read with the Module 7 rule of thumb (0.90–1.00 very high, 0.70–0.90 high, 0.50–0.70 moderate, 0.30–0.50 low, below 0.30 negligible).
